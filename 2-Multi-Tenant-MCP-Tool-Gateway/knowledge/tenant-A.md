# Tenant A payments, refunds, and returns

This policy belongs only to Tenant A Demo Store.

## Payment methods and failures

Tenant A accepts Visa, Mastercard, debit cards, and bank transfer. When a card payment
fails, confirm that the billing address and expiry date match, then wait 15 minutes before
one retry. After two failed attempts, contact Tenant A payment support and do not continue
retrying the card.

## Duplicate payments

A pending duplicate card authorization usually disappears within 24 hours. If both charges
settle, Tenant A treats the second charge as refundable. Do not submit another payment while
a duplicate-payment review is open.

## Return window

Unused physical items in original packaging can be returned within 30 calendar days of
delivery. Obtain a return authorization number before shipping. Original delivery fees are
refunded only when Tenant A made the fulfilment error.

## Refund timelines

Approved card and debit-card refunds return to the original payment method within five
business days. Bank-transfer refunds can take up to seven business days. Subscription
charges can be refunded within 14 days only when no premium feature was used during that
billing period; no partial-month refund is offered after the 14-day window.
