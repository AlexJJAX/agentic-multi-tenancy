# Tenant B device reset, update, and troubleshooting

This policy belongs only to Tenant B Device Support.

## Soft reset

Hold the device power button for 10 seconds, release it, and wait for the status light to
turn solid blue. A soft reset preserves Wi-Fi settings and paired accounts.

## Factory reset

Hold the recessed reset button for 15 seconds until the status light flashes amber. A factory
reset removes saved Wi-Fi settings and paired accounts, so reconnect the device after it
restarts.

## Firmware updates

Connect the device to Wi-Fi and charge the battery to at least 50 percent. Open Settings,
choose Device, then select Update Firmware. If the menu is unavailable, connect over USB,
open Beta Device Tool, and choose Manual Firmware Update.

## Connectivity troubleshooting

Restart the router, confirm it provides a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, and move the device within
10 metres of the router during setup. After three failed pairing attempts, perform a soft
reset before trying again.
