# Acme Payments support manual

## Payment methods

Acme accepts credit cards, debit cards, and ACH transfers. After one failed payment, verify
the billing address and card expiry before retrying after 15 minutes.

## Duplicate payments and refunds

Do not submit another payment when a duplicate charge appears. Pending authorisations can
disappear within 24 hours. A settled duplicate payment is refundable, and an approved refund
returns to the original payment method within five business days.

## Physical returns

Unused physical items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
A return authorisation number is required. Original shipping is refundable only when Acme
caused the fulfilment error.

## Subscription refunds

A subscription charge can be refunded within 14 days when no premium feature was used during
the billing period. Partial-month refunds are unavailable after that window.
