# Beta Devices support manual

## Soft and factory reset

For a soft reset, hold the power button for 10 seconds and wait for a solid blue light. For a
factory reset, hold the recessed reset button for 15 seconds until the light flashes amber.
A factory reset removes saved Wi-Fi settings and paired accounts.

## Firmware updates

Before an update, connect to Wi-Fi and charge the battery to at least 50 percent. From the
device menu, open Settings, choose Device, and select Update Firmware. The USB tool supports
a manual firmware update when the menu is unavailable.

## Connectivity

The setup network must use 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, and the device should remain within 10 metres of the
router. If pairing fails three times, perform a soft reset before trying again.

## Escalation

Contact device support only after recording the status-light colour, firmware version, and
the troubleshooting steps already attempted.
